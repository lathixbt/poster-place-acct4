A travel poster for a place that isn't there: **ORSOLIA**, the orchard that grows down from the sky. Fruit trees hang upside-down from the dawn clouds, their golden apples and plums glowing among the leaves, while tall wooden ladders rise from the meadow so villagers can climb up and pick fruit straight from the sky at sunrise. Below, a small village wakes in the green meadow as the first light turns the clouds pink — the promise of the poster is simple: come at dawn, climb a ladder, and pick your breakfast from the clouds.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` (1024x1024, PNG) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, validating size and dimensions first.
